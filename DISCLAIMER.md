# Disclaimer

MedChron Agent is a **portfolio and reference implementation**. It was built to show how an agent system for a legal and healthcare scenario can be designed and shipped.

- **Synthetic data only.** Every medical record, patient, provider and case in this repository is synthetic, produced by this project's own generator. None of it describes a real person. **Do not upload real patient data, protected health information (PHI) or client-confidential material** to any instance of this software.
- **Not a client deliverable.** This project was not built for, commissioned by or delivered to any law firm, healthcare organization or client.
- **Not legal or medical advice.** Outputs are machine-generated drafts that require review by qualified professionals. The system is designed to raise *questions for expert review*, not to reach conclusions about the standard of care, causation or liability.
- **Not a compliance certification.** The security and data-handling controls are modeled on HIPAA-style safeguards for demonstration. Compliance depends on the organization that operates a system, its policies and its vendor agreements, such as Business Associate Agreements. No claim of compliance is made.
