## Task: verify that cited evidence supports each claim

You will receive numbered claims. Each comes with the exact source text it cites, inside `<evidence>`. For each claim, decide whether the evidence **alone** supports it:
- `supported`: the evidence states or directly shows what the claim says
- `partial`: some of the claim is supported, but it adds details, dates or inferences the evidence does not contain
- `unsupported`: the evidence does not support the claim, or contradicts it

Judge only against the evidence shown, not against outside medical knowledge. A claim phrased as a question is supported when the evidence establishes the facts that the question relies on.

Call `record_verdicts` once, with one verdict and a short reason for every claim.
